SERIES CONSISTENCY CHECKER — KEEP YOUR SHOP ON-BRAND ACROSS 50+ LISTINGS

          
Informational panel — how to stay consistent across sessions, not a generator.

        

      

      

        
1 — Note Your Recipe
After you lock a style you love, click Copy Reference Block below. Save that block in a Google Doc called "My Shop Bible" — it contains your exact lighting rig, palette swatches, surface, mood, and prop family. Paste it verbatim into future GPT Image 2 prompts months later and you'll get the same photographer look.

        
2 — Re-paste Verbatim
Don't retype. Copy the Materials & Palette block from your Reference Block and paste it word-for-word into every new prompt: Surface: light oak — PBR wood, 0.7 roughness... + Palette: warm neutrals — swatches #FFF8E7.... One changed word = different photographer.

        
3 — Swatch Screenshot
Take a screenshot of your locked palette swatches (the 5 dots). Keep it on your desk. When you print or edit, eyedrop those exact hex codes in Canva/Photoshop for titles and overlays. Your shop's 50 listings will feel like one brand, not 50 experiments.
