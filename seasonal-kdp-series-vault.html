Complete guide

From first click to a linked series on Amazon. Every panel is collapsed: open what you need.

1How to generate your pages in ChatGPT
  1. Start one new ChatGPT chat per book (per volume in a series). Keeping a whole book in one chat helps the style stay consistent.
  2. Paste the Style Lock block first (Copy Style Lock button) and ask ChatGPT to keep it for every image that follows.
  3. Paste the page prompts one at a time, in order. Each prompt already contains the lock.
  4. Check each image: pure black lines on white, closed shapes, no gray or shading, no text, clean margins. Regenerate if needed.
  5. Download each PNG, name files by page number, and stay in the same chat for the next page.
  6. Generate covers in a NEW chat: they are full colour and must not inherit the black-and-white rules.
Opening message (uses the Style Lock of the tab you are on)

I am making a coloring book and will send one page prompt at a time. Use the STYLE LOCK below for every image in this chat and never change it from page to page. Reply only "Ready" and wait for my first page prompt. [your Style Lock block is added here]

Fix messages (click to copy, paste in the same chat)
Gray or shaded lines

Image sizes available depend on your ChatGPT plan. If the ratio is not exactly your trim size, scale or crop it in Canva. Always look at every image before using it.
2Building a series (Series Builder workflow)
  1. Pick a collection and the number of volumes. Press Generate Series.
  2. Read the Series Lock: one line style for every page of every volume, and one Series Cover DNA (layout, lettering, border, badge, art style) for every cover.
  3. Each volume has its own sub-theme (a "lens" such as Moonlit Nights), its own featured subjects and its own accent colour. Scenes never repeat anywhere in the series.
  4. Generate each volume's pages in its own ChatGPT chat. Generate each volume's covers in a new chat and keep the Cover DNA text unchanged so the covers match.
  5. Use Export Volume N to keep each volume's prompt pack, or Export Whole Series for everything.
  6. Build and upload each volume as its own KDP book, then link them (steps are in the series listing block).
Tip: if you change the Series Lock (Re-roll Series Style), all covers and pages prompts update together, so re-generate every volume to keep the family consistent.
3Book Extras: the pages every coloring book needs
  • Title page and "This book belongs to" pages are image prompts in your locked style. The only text allowed in them is the text the prompt names. AI tools often misspell lettering: if it happens, regenerate or add the text in Canva.
  • Colour-test page: a sheet of empty shapes so colourists can test markers before starting.
  • Copyright page: plain sample text with [square-bracket] items for your name and year. It is not legal advice.
  • Thank-you page: thanks readers and asks for an honest review. It offers no reward and does not pressure anyone, because incentivised reviews break Amazon's rules.
  • Interior order: title page, belongs-to page, colour test, coloring pages (each with a blank page behind), thank-you page.
4Assemble your interior
  1. In Canva (or similar), create a custom design at your exact trim size in inches.
  2. Follow the recommended interior order. Keep every image inside the margins (about 0.5 in clear).
  3. Add a blank page behind each coloring page so ink cannot bleed through.
  4. KDP paperbacks need at least 24 pages in total. Blank pages count toward the total.
  5. Export as PDF Print at 300 DPI, then flip through every page.

No. Close the tab and everything is gone. Use the export buttons first.
