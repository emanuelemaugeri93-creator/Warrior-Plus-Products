📐 How to Save Your Pages at 300 DPI
  1. In ChatGPT, generate the image using the prompt above.
  2. Right-click the generated image → "Save image" (this gives you the native high-res export, not a screenshot).
  3. Open the file in Canva or Photoshop and check/set the document to 300 DPI at your exact trim size.
  4. If the export is under 300 DPI at your trim size, upscale with Canva's "Enhance" or Photoshop's "Preserve Details 2.0".
  5. Repeat the prompt for each page you need — each generation will be slightly different, perfect for unique books!
