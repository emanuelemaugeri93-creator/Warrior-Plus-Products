Guide

Start here: 5 steps

  1. Fill the Active Book (left panel): theme, audience, trim size, pages, title and main keyword. Every module reads from it.
  2. Plan: the Planner shows when to start so the book is live before the season.
  3. Build: make puzzle pages in Activity Studio, then upload your coloring images in Interior Builder and export the interior PDF.
  4. Launch: Listing Lab writes title, keywords and description options. Thumbnail Bench shows how your cover reads at small size.
  5. Promote: Promo Pack writes posts and captions. Profit & Ads does the royalty and ad-budget arithmetic.

What each module does

Planner
Season dates, a back-planned calendar and the status of each deadline: on track, window closing, late.
Activity Studio
Word search, maze, sudoku, scramble and lettering pages generated by code. Mazes and sudoku are verified solvable, word searches contain every word. Add pages to the queue.
Interior Builder
Upload your images, choose where activity pages go, add title and copyright pages, then export a PDF at the exact trim size with safe margins and a mirrored gutter. Also has the cover wrap calculator.
Listing Lab
Title and subtitle options, a 7-slot keyword packer (50 characters each), description builder and a rules check.
Thumbnail Bench
Checks your cover at the small sizes shoppers see.
Promo Pack
Captions, hooks, video angles and hashtags for the book.
Profit & Ads
Royalty calculator and an ads plan from numbers you enter.

Good to know

  • Everything stays in your browser. Images are never uploaded and nothing is saved when you close the tab. Export your PDF before you leave.
  • 300 DPI export takes time. A book with 60 images can take a couple of minutes. Keep the tab open and in front.
  • Keyword Packer works best with real suggestions. Paste phrases you saw in Amazon search suggestions. Seeds alone give rougher slots.
  • No data, no promises. The tool has no access to Amazon search volume or sales. It makes no income claims and no ranking promises.
  • KDP changes its rules. Every limit sits in one config at the top of the script. Verify margins, minimum pages and spine factors in your KDP dashboard.
  • Make each book distinct. KDP can reject repetitive or low-value content. Disclose AI-generated content when you upload.
